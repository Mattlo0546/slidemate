---
Use when a slide covers events, periods, theories of society or politics, or scholars' interpretations.

Question moves:
- Causes: "What were the most important causes of <event>, and how do historians weigh them?"
- Significance: "Why does <event / idea> matter for what came after?"
- Interpretations: "How do <scholar A> and <scholar B> disagree about <topic>?"
- Evidence: "What evidence supports the claim that <claim>, and what are its limits?"
- Concepts in use: "How is <concept> being defined here, and how would it apply to <case>?"
- Essay angle: "What would a strong essay argument about <topic> look like, and what's the best counter-argument?"

Good: "How do structuralist and intentionalist historians explain the escalation differently?"
Weak: "What happened in 1914?"

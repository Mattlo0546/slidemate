---
title: Reading literary and primary texts
tags: literature, text, novel, poem, reading, close-reading, theme, form, author, criticism, language
summary: For slides on texts: ask about how form and language create meaning, themes, context and critical readings.
---
Use when a slide quotes or discusses a literary or primary text, an author or a critical approach.

Question moves:
- Close reading: "How does the wording / imagery in <quote> create <effect>?"
- Form: "Why might the author use <form, structure or narrator> here?"
- Theme: "How does <passage> develop the theme of <theme>?"
- Context: "How does <historical / biographical context> change how we read this?"
- Critical lens: "What would a <feminist / postcolonial / formalist> reading notice here?"
- Compare: "How does this treatment of <theme> differ from <other text>?"

Good: "Why does the shift from first to third person in this chapter matter for how we judge the narrator?"
Weak: "What is this poem about?"

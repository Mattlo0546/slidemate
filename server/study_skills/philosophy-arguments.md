Use when a slide presents a philosopher's position, an argument, an objection, a thought experiment or a distinction.

Question moves:
- Reconstruct: "What are the premises of <philosopher>'s argument for <conclusion>, in order?"
- Weakest premise: "Which premise of <argument> is the most controversial, and why?"
- Objection and reply: "How would <philosopher> reply to the objection that <objection>?"
- Thought experiment: "What exactly is <thought experiment> supposed to show, and what intuition does it rely on?"
- Distinguish: "What's the difference between <concept A> and <concept B> as used here?"
- Compare positions: "Where do <position A> and <position B> actually disagree?"
- Apply: "What would <theory> say about <new case>?"

Good: "Which premise of Searle's Chinese Room would a systems-reply defender reject, and why?"
Weak: "What did Searle think?" / "Is this right?"

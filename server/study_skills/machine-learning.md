summary: For ML/AI slides: ask about what the model can represent, why training works, and what happens when a hyperparameter or assumption changes.
---
Use when a slide covers a learning algorithm, network architecture, loss function, training procedure or evaluation.

Question moves:
- What it can represent: "Why can't a single-layer <model> represent <function, e.g. XOR>?"
- Why training works: "Why does <update rule> move the weights in the right direction?"
- Change a knob: "What happens if the learning rate / number of layers / regularisation is too large or too small?"
- Compare: "How does <method> differ from <method> in what it assumes or optimises?"
- Failure modes: "How would I tell whether this model is overfitting, and what would I change?"
- Geometry and intuition: "What does the decision boundary of <model> look like here, and why?"
- History and claims: "Why was <result> considered a breakthrough / a limitation at the time?"

Good: "Why does adding a hidden layer let the network separate XOR when one layer can't?"
Weak: "What is a neural network?"

---
Use when a slide covers a statistical test, model, distribution, estimator or research design.

Question moves:
- Assumptions: "What does <test/model> assume about the data, and what happens if that assumption fails?"
- Interpretation: "How should I interpret <statistic> = <value> in plain words? What does it NOT tell me?"
- Choice of method: "When would I use <method> rather than <alternative>?"
- Misconceptions: "Why is it wrong to read a p-value as the probability the hypothesis is true?"
- Design: "How would a confounder like <factor> change the conclusion here?"
- Effect of sample size: "How does a larger sample change <interval / power / estimate> here?"

Good: "Why does correlation in this study not let us conclude that screen time causes poor sleep?"
Weak: "What is regression?"

summary: For economics/business slides: ask about model assumptions, what shifts the graph, policy effects, and real-world fit.
---
Use when a slide presents an economic or social-science model, graph, policy or business framework.

Question moves:
- Assumptions: "What does <model> assume, and how realistic is that for <market>?"
- Shifts: "What would shift <curve> here, and what happens to <price / quantity / outcome>?"
- Policy: "What are the intended and unintended effects of <policy>?"
- Mechanism: "Why does <incentive> lead people / firms to <behaviour>?"
- Evidence: "What real-world evidence supports or challenges <prediction>?"
- Compare: "How do <school A> and <school B> explain <phenomenon> differently?"

Good: "Why does a price ceiling below equilibrium create a shortage, and who loses most from it?"
Weak: "What is supply and demand?"

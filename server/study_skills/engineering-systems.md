---
title: Engineering systems and design trade-offs
tags: engineering, robotics, actuator, sensor, control, circuit, mechanism, system, design, trade-off, mechanical, electrical
summary: For engineering slides: ask how a mechanism works, why one design was chosen over another, and how it fails.
---
Use when a slide covers a component, mechanism, circuit, control system, or a design choice.

Question moves:
- Mechanism: "How does <component> actually convert <input> into <output>?"
- Trade-off: "Why would I choose <option A> over <option B> for <application> (cost, torque, precision, power)?"
- Cause and effect: "What happens to <output> if <parameter> increases?"
- Failure: "What are the typical failure modes of <system>, and how are they detected?"
- Real example: "Where is <technique> used in real robots / devices, and why there?"
- Integration: "How does <part> interact with <other part> in the full system?"

Good: "Why do robot arms often use harmonic drives instead of planetary gearboxes in the joints?"
Weak: "What is an actuator?"

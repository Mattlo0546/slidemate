---
title: Natural sciences: mechanisms and evidence
tags: science, biology, chemistry, physics, mechanism, experiment, reaction, cell, energy, evidence, model
summary: For science slides: ask about the underlying mechanism, the evidence for it, and predictions when conditions change.
---
Use when a slide explains a natural process, mechanism, model or experiment.

Question moves:
- Mechanism: "What is happening at the <molecular / particle / cellular> level when <process> occurs?"
- Prediction: "What would happen to <system> if <condition> changed?"
- Evidence: "What experiment showed <finding>, and what was the control?"
- Model limits: "Where does <model> stop being accurate?"
- Compare: "How is <process A> different from <process B>, and why does the difference matter?"
- Connect scales: "How does <micro-level fact> explain <macro-level observation>?"

Good: "Why does increasing temperature speed up this reaction at first but then slow the enzyme down?"
Weak: "What is photosynthesis?"

summary: For slides with worked examples or calculations: ask how to choose the method, why each step, and how to check the answer.
---
Use when a slide shows a worked example, a numerical calculation or an exercise the student will have to do themselves.

Question moves:
- Choosing the method: "How do I know to use <method> for this kind of problem rather than <alternative>?"
- The key step: "Why do we <step> at this point in the example?"
- Checking: "How could I check the answer to this example is right (units, limits, order of magnitude)?"
- Variation: "How would the solution change if <given quantity> were different / <condition> didn't hold?"
- Common traps: "What's the most common mistake students make on problems like this?"
- Transfer: "What would an exam question on this look like, and what's the first thing I should write?"

Good: "Why do we take moments about the hinge in this example instead of the centre of mass?"
Weak: "Can you solve this?" / "What's the answer?"

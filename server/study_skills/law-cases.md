tags: law, case, statute, legal, rule, precedent, judgment, contract, tort, rights, court
summary: For law slides: ask what rule a case established, why the court decided as it did, and how the rule applies to new facts.
---
Use when a slide covers a legal rule, statute, case, or doctrine.

Question moves:
- Ratio: "What rule did <case> establish, and what facts were essential to it?"
- Reasoning: "Why did the court reject <argument> in <case>?"
- Apply: "How would <rule> apply if <facts changed>?"
- Distinguish: "How could a later court distinguish <case>?"
- Tension: "How does <case> fit with <other case or statute>?"
- Policy: "What policy reasons support or criticise this rule?"

Good: "Why wasn't the advertisement in Carlill a mere invitation to treat, and would an online ad be different?"
Weak: "What happened in this case?"

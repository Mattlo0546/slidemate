---
title: Exam focus and common confusions
tags: general, exam, revision, misconception, confusion, compare, definition, key-idea, assessment
summary: For any subject: ask about what the lecturer stressed, ideas students confuse, and how a topic is examined.
---
Use for any lecture when the slide holds a key idea, a flagged exam point or two easily confused concepts.

Question moves:
- Emphasis: "Why did the lecturer stress <point>? What's the key thing to take away?"
- Confusable pair: "What's the difference between <A> and <B>, and how do I avoid mixing them up?"
- Misconception: "What's the most common misunderstanding about <idea>?"
- Exam form: "How might <topic> be asked about in the exam, and what would a full-marks answer include?"
- Big picture: "Where does <slide topic> fit in the argument of the whole lecture?"

Good: "What's the difference between validity and soundness, and why do students mix them up?"
Weak: "Is this important?"

Batch versus stochastic. Batch gradient descent computes the gradient over the entire dataset every single step. Exact, but slow when you have millions of examples. Stochastic gradient descent uses one example at a time. Very noisy, but very fast. And what everyone actually uses is mini-batch, a few dozen examples at a time. A fun fact: the noise from stochastic gradients can actually help, because it can kick you out of shallow local minima.

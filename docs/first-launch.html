SlideMate First launch · macOS 13+

Let's open SlideMate.

Your Mac may ask you to approve SlideMate before its welcome screen appears. Keep this guide open while you get set up.

  1. Move SlideMate to Applications

    Drag SlideMate.app into Applications. In a DMG, use the Applications shortcut beside the app. From a ZIP, use the Applications folder in Finder.

  2. Try opening it once

    Open SlideMate from Applications. If you see “Apple could not verify” or “unidentified developer”, dismiss the alert with Done, OK or Cancel. If the app opens normally, go to step 4.

  3. Only if macOS blocked it

    Approve SlideMate in Settings

    For a copy you trust from SlideMate's official GitHub releases, open the Apple menu and follow this path:

    On your Mac, go to

    System Settings→ Privacy & Security→ Security→ Open Anyway

    Scroll down to the message about SlideMate. Some macOS versions show Open before Open Anyway. Confirm Open if another alert appears, and enter your Mac login password if asked.

    Your Mac remembers this approval for this copy of the app.

  4. Continue in SlideMate

    Choose your AI and sign in, then connect your university or open a PDF. If the app hasn't opened yet, open SlideMate from Applications again.

I can't see Open Anyway

Try opening SlideMate from Applications again, dismiss the alert, then return to Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. The option appears for about an hour after an attempted launch. On a university or work Mac, your IT team may need to approve it.

I'm using macOS 13 or 14

You can also Control-click SlideMate in Finder, choose Open, then confirm Open.

The warning is different, or the app is missing

If macOS says the app “will damage your computer” or “contains malware”, stop and report the exact warning. These steps are for verification alerts. If you chose Move to Bin, download a fresh copy from the official release and move it to Applications again.

Do I need Microphone or Accessibility access?

Those are separate. Microphone access is requested when you record a lecture. Accessibility is only needed for the optional AirDrop auto-select feature. Neither is needed just to open SlideMate.
